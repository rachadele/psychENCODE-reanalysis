# Author subtypes vs pooled author types in DE

We asked whether the author subtypes that our pipeline pools (five IT types into L2/3-6 IT, Lamp5 with Lamp5_Lhx6, Sst with Sst_Chodl) gave the same DE as their pooled group. We reused existing limma-voom results: 27 unmerged author types and 21 merged author types (`results_v2.1.2_limma/merged_author_2026-11-09`). For the eight Figure 4 contrasts, `subtype_vs_pooled.py` computed Spearman ρ of log2FC and of t over shared genes between each subtype and its merged group, between subtypes of a group, between each subtype and author types outside its group (same class, and all), and between the merged group and our label. We averaged pairs within each contrast and report mean ± SD over contrasts (`summary.tsv`; all pairs in `pairwise_rho.tsv`). Cells per donor was the median over DE donors (`cells_per_donor.tsv`). The merged-vs-ours values matched `de_agreement/celltype_merged.tsv`.

Subtypes agreed with their merged group at ρ = 0.76 ± 0.08 (IT), 0.86 ± 0.06 (Lamp5) and 0.68 ± 0.12 (Sst), and with each other at 0.66, 0.64 and 0.32. Same-class unrelated types gave 0.56, 0.54 and 0.47. Agreement fell with subtype size: Sst_Chodl (4 cells per donor) agreed with Sst at 0.32. `manifest.json` holds sha256 and timestamps.
